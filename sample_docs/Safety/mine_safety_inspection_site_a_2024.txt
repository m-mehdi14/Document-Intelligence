Report ID: MSA-2024-03-012
Date: 2024-03-15
Site: Site A (North Sector)

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
A full safety inspection was conducted at Site A on 15 March 2024. Overall compliance with MOL Group safety standards is satisfactory. Two minor non-conformities were identified and corrective actions have been scheduled.

FINDINGS
1. Personal protective equipment (PPE): All personnel observed were wearing appropriate PPE (helmets, high-vis vests, safety boots). No violations.
2. Blasting area signage: Signage at the eastern blasting zone was faded. Recommendation: replace within 30 days.
3. Emergency evacuation routes: Routes are clearly marked and unobstructed. Evacuation drill scheduled for April 2024.
4. Vehicle and machinery: All inspected vehicles had valid safety certifications. One haul truck (ID 447) is due for brake inspection by end of month.

CONCLUSIONS
Site A remains in good standing. The latest mine safety inspection report for Site A confirms that no critical issues were found. Follow-up inspection in Q3 2024.
