STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — BLASTING OPERATIONS
Document: SOP-BLAST-001
Version: 2.1
Last updated: 2023-06-20
Department: Mining Operations

1. SCOPE
This SOP applies to all blasting activities at MOL Group mining sites. All personnel involved in blast design, charging, or initiation must be trained and authorized.

2. PREREQUISITES
- Valid blasting permit and site-specific risk assessment.
- Blasting SOPs updated after 2022 must be used; earlier versions are obsolete.
- Pre-blast inspection of area and exclusion zone.

3. PROCEDURE
3.1 Design
- Blast design must be approved by site engineer.
- Maximum charge per delay and total charge limits as per local regulations.

3.2 Charging
- Only authorized personnel in charging area.
- Use designated explosives and initiation system.
- Record batch and lot numbers.

3.3 Initiation
- Clear exclusion zone; all personnel accounted for.
- Sound warning signals before blast.
- Post-blast inspection before re-entry.

4. RECORDS
- Blast log (time, location, design, results) to be retained for 7 years.
- Any incident must be reported within 24 hours.

5. REFERENCES
- Mine Safety Handbook, Section 4
- Environmental impact report for Project X (blasting chapter)

For blasting SOPs updated after 2022, always refer to this document (v2.1, 2023).
